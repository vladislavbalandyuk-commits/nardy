Long nardy rules

Rules

  1. Each player has 15 checkers on the “head”. Both move the same way round; the heads are opposite.
  2. No hitting. You cannot land on a point your opponent holds.
  3. One checker leaves the head per turn. On the first roll with 6-6, 4-4 or 3-3 — two.
  4. A double is played four times. You must use both dice; if only one fits, use the larger.
  5. You cannot build 6 points in a row unless at least one opposing checker is ahead of them.
  6. Bear off once all 15 are home. Winning before the opponent bears off any — “mars”, 2 points.

Terms

Head
point 24, where all your checkers start.
Home
the last six points (1–6); checkers are borne off from here.
Mars
a win when the opponent has borne off nothing — 2 points.

Questions

How is long nardy different from backgammon?
There is no hitting: both players move in the same direction and all checkers start from the head.
How long is a game?
Usually 10–20 minutes; blitz with 10 seconds per move is faster.
