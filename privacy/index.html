Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Long Nardy (“the game”, nardy-nine.vercel.app, also played inside Telegram and on game portals such as CrazyGames) is a free online board game. This page explains what we store and why.

What we store

  • Your game account: an internal player ID, the nickname you choose (or a generated guest name), your avatar choice, rating, games played and won, coins, items, achievements and settings.
  • Sign-in data: if you sign in with a nickname and password, the password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. If you sign in with Telegram or CrazyGames, we keep the ID that service gives us so you get the same account next time. We do not receive your Telegram or CrazyGames password.
  • Games: the moves of games you play (for replays, ratings and fair-play checks), and reports you send about other players.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the push subscription your browser gives us, or your Telegram chat with our bot.
  • On your device: settings and progress are also kept in your browser's local storage.

What we do not do

  • We do not show third-party ads inside the game and do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location.

Where it is kept

The game runs on Vercel (website) and Supabase (database, sign-in and game server). On game portals the portal itself may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Voice chat

Optional voice chat between two players is peer-to-peer and is not recorded or stored by us. You can block or report a player from the in-game menu.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can change your nickname, turn off notifications or stop playing at any time. To delete your account and its data, message the game's admin via our Telegram bot @dovgi_nardy_bot; we will delete it.

Політика конфіденційності

Ми зберігаємо лише те, що потрібно для гри: ідентифікатор гравця, нік (або згенероване ім’я гостя), аватар, рейтинг, статистику, монети, предмети, досягнення й налаштування; ходи партій (для повторів, рейтингу й перевірки чесної гри) і скарги на гравців. Пароль зберігається лише у вигляді захищеного хешу; при вході через Telegram або CrazyGames ми зберігаємо лише ідентифікатор від цього сервісу. Ми не показуємо сторонньої реклами, не продаємо даних і не просимо справжнє ім’я, пошту, телефон чи адресу. Дані зберігаються у Vercel і Supabase. Голосовий чат іде напряму між гравцями й не записується. Щоб видалити акаунт, напишіть адміну через бота @dovgi_nardy_bot.
